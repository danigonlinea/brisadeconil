Every guest asks me this on arrival, towel already half folded: *«so, which is the best beach?»*.

And my answer is always the same: **it depends who you are today**. A family with two little ones and a pushchair doesn't want the same beach as a couple after silence, or a surfer with the Levante wind promising waves. Conil has fourteen kilometres of coast — the problem isn't finding a beach, it's choosing well.

## With family: Los Bateles and La Fontanilla

**Los Bateles** is our town beach, straight down from the old quarter. Wide sand, beach bars where you can have lunch without abandoning your parasol, lifeguard, showers — and that supreme comfort: if you forget something back at the apartment, you're up and back in ten minutes. In August it's lively — I call it "towel to towel" — but that's exactly why it works with kids: you're never far from anything.

**La Fontanilla**, a walk further north, is its slightly calmer sister. Same good services and long-standing beach bars, but less crowded than Bateles in peak August. Plenty of local families alternate between the two depending on the day.

With small children, my neighbourly advice: early morning or late afternoon. From eleven to four the sun punishes and the town beaches are packed; at ten in the morning it's another world.

## If you want quiet: the coves of Roche

North, past Fuente del Gallo, something else begins: **the cliffs and the coves**. Roqueo, Camarón, Puntalejos, Cala Lee… finer sand, water that looks like a different sea, and the kind of silence that's worth gold in August.

But let me be blunt, because I'd rather have happy guests than surprised ones: **these coves are not for pushchairs or heavy gear**. Access is on foot along paths and steps from the area's car parks, some descents are steep, and at high tide they lose much of their sand. In exchange you get rock pools as the tide goes out, travel-brochure clarity and an afternoon you'll remember.

Bring water, a hat and patience on the way down. The prize is paid for in sweat.

## With a board: El Palmar and the Salado river mouth

For surfers the address is clear: **El Palmar**, ten minutes by car, is the surf capital around here — consistent waves, surf schools, laid-back beach bars. Within Conil itself, the **mouth of the Río Salado** works for kite and windsurf.

A word on wind, because here it decides everything: **Levante** (easterly) brings waves and whips up sand; **Poniente** (westerly) brings calmer water and the golden sunsets. Ask me on arrival what's forecast for the week — that's how I plan my own days.

## With a dog

This is the most frequent question in spring and autumn. Rules change by season and by stretch of coast, so here's the honest answer: **outside high season it's much easier**, and during the summer months restrictions apply on the town beaches. If you're coming with a dog, write to us beforehand — we'll tell you how it actually stands for your dates instead of letting you arrive on outdated information.

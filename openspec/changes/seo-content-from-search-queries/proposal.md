# SEO Content from Search Queries — Proposal

## Why

Google search queries reveal exactly what potential guests look for before contacting us. Analysis of 10 real queries shows:
- 4 queries about "mercado de abastos" (local market) — highest repeat intent
- 2 queries about dogs on beaches — underserved niche
- 1 German query ("strände conil") — confirms DE traffic
- 1 direct brand search ("casa brisa conil") — brand awareness working
- Queries about specific beaches (El Palmar, Roche) — matches existing blog posts

The blog is already the top acquisition channel. Each post acts as a lead magnet: search → read → discover apartment → contact. We need to double down on this pattern.

## What changes

1. **New blog posts** targeting high-intent queries:
   - "Perros en las playas de Conil" (dog-friendly beaches — 2 queries, zero content)
   - "Mercado de Abastos de Conil: guía completa" (expand existing post with more practical info + stronger booking CTA)
   - "Mirador de Roche: vistas y cómo llegar" (optimize existing post for this query)
   - "Playas de Conil por perfil" (already exists — optimize title/meta for "playas en conil")

2. **Internal linking** from every blog post to the apartment/contact section with contextually relevant anchor text.

3. **FAQ additions** answering questions that appear in search queries (dog policy, market hours, beach accessibility).

4. **Landing page optimization** — ensure "alojamiento-cerca-playa-conil" and "apartamento-vacacional-conil" pages answer the specific questions behind these searches.

## Non-goals

- No new page templates or components
- No changes to the booking flow or form
- No new imagery (use existing gallery photos)
- No changes to analytics/tracking (already implemented)

## Risks

- Over-optimizing for search queries could make content feel mechanical — keep natural host voice
- Too many posts too thin — better fewer, comprehensive posts
- German translations must be natural, not machine-translated

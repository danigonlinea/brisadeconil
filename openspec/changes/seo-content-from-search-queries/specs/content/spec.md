# Content — Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### CNT-1: Dog-friendly beach guide
The system SHALL provide a blog post (es/en/de) covering dog-friendly beaches near Conil, including:
- Which beaches allow dogs and in which season
- Rules and regulations (leash requirements, restricted hours)
- How to reach each beach from the apartment
- A CTA to check availability at the end

### CNT-2: Market guide expansion
The existing "Mercado de Abastos" blog post SHALL be expanded to include:
- Opening hours and days
- What to buy (fish, produce, local specialties)
- Tips from the host (personal recommendations)
- Location relative to the apartment (walking distance)
- Stronger booking CTA with context ("stay steps away from the market")

### CNT-3: Beach-by-profile optimization
The existing "Playas de Conil por perfil" post SHALL be optimized for the query "playas en conil de la frontera" by:
- Adding the exact phrase to the meta description
- Adding an H2 with "Las mejores playas de Conil de la Frontera"
- Ensuring internal links use descriptive anchor text

### CNT-4: Internal linking from blog to booking
Every blog post SHALL include at least one contextual internal link to the apartment or contact section, using anchor text that matches the search intent (e.g. "reservar el apartamento", "consultar disponibilidad").

### CNT-5: FAQ additions from search queries
The FAQ SHALL include answers to:
- Are dogs allowed in the apartment?
- Is there parking near the market/beaches?
- What's the best way to get to Roche beach?

## MODIFIED Requirements

### CNT-6: Roche blog post (existing)
The existing "Senderismo acantilados de Roche" post SHALL be optimized for the query "mirador de playa de roche" by:
- Adding "mirador" to the title or first paragraph
- Including a photo of the viewpoint
- Adding a CTA at the end

## REMOVED Requirements

None.
